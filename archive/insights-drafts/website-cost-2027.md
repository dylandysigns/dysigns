## The short answer

For budgeting, treat the 2026 ranges as your starting point for 2027: a professionally built business website typically sits roughly between €2,500 and €7,000, a professionally designed Shopify store from around €6,000 to €8,000, and a custom project between €10,000 and €30,000 or more. These are general market indications, not a DYSIGNS price list and not a forecast. The full breakdown is in [what a website costs in 2026](/insights/website-cost-2026).

What changes in 2027 is less the headline number and more what that number has to cover.

## What is changing the price in 2027

**AI tooling speeds up routine work, not the hard parts.** Drafting layouts, boilerplate code and first-pass copy is faster than it was. Strategy, design decisions, integrations and getting a checkout to convert still take the same thinking, so a good project does not simply become much cheaper. What you pay for shifts toward judgement and away from production.

**The cheap end gets cheaper, the gap widens.** AI website builders now cost roughly €5 to €50 per month. That makes a very simple site almost free, and makes the difference with a custom build clearer: control over speed, custom functionality and how well you can be found.

**Accessibility is a requirement, not an extra.** The European Accessibility Act has applied since 28 June 2025 and covers, among others, webshops and e-commerce services. Micro-enterprises can fall outside it, so check whether it applies to you. For projects that are in scope, accessibility is cheapest when it is built in from the design phase rather than retrofitted.

**Being found now includes AI search.** Clean markup, structured data, fast pages and clearly written content matter for Google, and increasingly for AI assistants that summarise sources. This is technical groundwork that costs less during the build than after it.

## How to budget for 2027

- **Separate build from run.** Budget the launch (design and development) and the first year of running (hosting, maintenance, content, marketing) as two lines. Typical market indications for running costs are €10 to €50 per month for hosting and €50 to €200 per month for maintenance when outsourced.
- **Decide your platform early.** A Shopify store, a modified theme and a headless build sit at very different price points, so the platform choice drives the budget more than the page count.
- **Plan for change.** A site that can be improved step by step costs less over three years than one that needs a full rebuild after eighteen months.
- **Spend where it earns.** If the site has to bring in customers, reserve budget for conversion work and content after launch, not only for the launch itself.

## Frequently asked questions

### Will websites be cheaper in 2027?

Simple sites will keep getting cheaper because of templates and AI builders. A custom, professionally designed site is unlikely to drop much, since the costs are in strategy, design, integrations and testing rather than typing code.

### Should I wait until 2027 to build my website?

Usually not. If your current site is costing you customers, waiting costs more than building now. A well-built site can be improved over time, so launching now does not lock you out of later changes.

### Does my webshop need to be accessible?

The European Accessibility Act has applied since 28 June 2025 to many webshops and online services in the EU. Micro-enterprises may be exempt, so check your own situation. Either way, an accessible site is easier to use for everyone, and it is cheapest to plan for from the start.

### What should I budget for a Shopify store in 2027?

Use the 2026 ranges as a starting point: roughly €6,000 to €8,000 for a professionally designed store and €10,000 to €30,000 or more for a custom project, plus the platform subscription and apps. Scope and integrations decide where you land.
