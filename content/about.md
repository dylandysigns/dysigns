## How we work

DYSIGNS started from UX and UI design, driven by the ambition to grow across multiple design disciplines. A recurring problem kept coming up with clients: waiting too long for changes and lacking clarity in the process, which cost momentum and missed opportunities. That insight became the foundation for how DYSIGNS works today: transparent, fast, with short feedback loops, clear communication, and one fixed point of contact instead of a rotating team.
