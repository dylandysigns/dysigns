## When a theme is the better answer

Headless is a trade: more freedom in the front end, in exchange for more technology to maintain. Modern Shopify themes can do far more than their reputation suggests, and speed problems come more often from heavy apps and images than from the theme itself. If your store is mainly slow or looks generic, [a custom theme](/shopify-store-design) usually goes live sooner and costs less to maintain. We say so out loud when that is the better answer for you.

## Frequently asked questions

### What is headless Shopify?

With headless Shopify, Shopify keeps running products, inventory, payments and checkout, but the front end your customers see is a separate application that talks to Shopify through the Storefront API. You get freedom in design and technology, and you take on responsibility for that front end.

### Is headless better for speed and SEO?

Not automatically. A well-built headless storefront can be very fast, but a poorly built one loses rankings through missing redirects, client-side rendering or broken structured data. That is why we check URLs, redirects and metadata up front, and test everything before launch.

### What is the difference between Hydrogen and a theme?

A theme runs inside Shopify and you customise it with sections and blocks. Hydrogen is a library from Shopify for building your own storefront, with its own hosting and release process. It gives more freedom, but also more to manage.

### Will my Shopify apps still work on a headless storefront?

Apps that work in the background, such as inventory or accounting connections, usually keep working. Apps that add something to your theme, such as review widgets, need an API version or custom work. We map that out before the project starts.

### Who maintains the storefront after launch?

A headless front end is software and needs maintenance: framework and API updates, security and new features. Your own developers can take it over with our documentation, or we stay on as your development partner. We advise against launching without someone being responsible.
