## What this includes

### Brand and structure

We translate your brand into a store with a clear structure: what shoppers see first, how they search and where they decide.

### Product and collection pages

Pages that show products as they are, with fast navigation, good imagery and everything a shopper wants to know before buying.

### Cart and checkout

The route from add to cart to paying, as short and clear as possible, with the payment methods your customers expect.

### Sections your team manages

A setup with sections and blocks in the Shopify theme, so your team edits campaigns and pages without a developer.

## Why design and development in one team

A design dreamed up in one agency and built in another often loses exactly the details that made it good along the way. At DYSIGNS design and development sit at the same table. That cuts handover, keeps the design buildable and makes building faster. Read more about the design side under [UX/UI design](/ux-ui-design) and [branding](/branding).

## Frequently asked questions

### What is the difference with a standard theme?

A standard theme is a good start, but anyone can have it. With Shopify store design we design the structure, sections and details around your brand and products, so your store does not look like someone else's.

### Do you build in a theme or headless?

Mostly in a Shopify theme with custom sections, because it goes live sooner and is easier to manage. If that does not fit, we discuss [headless Shopify](/headless-shopify).

### Can you redesign my existing store?

Yes. Often an existing store can be improved without rebuilding everything, depending on the state of your theme and apps.

### Will the store work well on mobile?

Yes. We design mobile first, because many of your shoppers arrive on their phones, and we test on real devices.

### Will I manage the store myself?

Yes. At handover you get training, so you manage products, orders and content yourself.
