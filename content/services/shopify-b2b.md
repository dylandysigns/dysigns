## What this includes

### Business pricing and catalogues

Separate prices and ranges per customer or customer group, so every business buyer sees what applies to them.

### Company accounts

One login per company, with several users and clear permissions, so buyers can order on their own.

### An ordering experience that works

Fast reordering, order lists and larger quantities, designed so a buyer does not have to hunt through a consumer store.

### Invoices and payment terms

Paying on invoice and fixed agreements, connected to your accounting and, if needed, your inventory system.

## When B2B on Shopify fits

Shopify has built-in B2B features, such as company profiles, separate catalogues and price lists, so you can often combine B2C and B2B in one store. That works well for most wholesalers and brands that supply shops. If your pricing agreements are very complex or everything hangs on your own ERP, we first look together at whether Shopify is the right place for it. We are honest about that.

## Frequently asked questions

### Can I combine B2B and B2C in the same Shopify store?

Often, yes. With company profiles and separate catalogues, business customers see their own prices and range while consumers keep using the regular store.

### Can business customers pay on invoice?

Yes. Payment terms such as paying on invoice can be set up, and we connect them to your accounting so invoicing adds up.

### Can customers place repeat orders quickly?

Yes. We design order lists and repeat orders so a buyer reorders what they need every month in a few clicks.

### How do separate prices per customer work?

You link a customer to a catalogue or price list, and after logging in they see their own prices. We set this up and explain how to manage it yourself.

### Can the store talk to my inventory or ERP?

That is possible through an integration. What it takes depends on your system. See [Shopify integrations](/shopify-integrations) for how we approach that.

### What does a Shopify B2B store cost?

It depends on the number of customer groups, integrations and custom work. A range is in [what a website or store costs](/insights/website-cost), and a first conversation is free and without obligation.
