## What this includes

### Website

A custom business website, fitted to your brand, fast on mobile and readable for search engines from the first release.

### Shopify store

A Shopify store with a custom theme, integrations and Shopify Markets if you also sell outside the Netherlands, built by an official Shopify Partner.

### Apps and platforms

UX/UI design and development for apps, dashboards and portals, from the first design to the live version.

## Why a team close to Amsterdam

Looking for a web design agency for your website or store in Amsterdam? We are based in Almere and regularly work in Amsterdam, so meeting at your place is easy to arrange. We know the city up close: for BIYU we worked from their office on the Overtoom on the app, the website and the dashboard behind their rental service. Most of the work itself runs remotely, with short lines and one fixed point of contact, in Dutch or English.

## Who it is for

For Amsterdam brands, founders and scale-ups who want a website, store or app that goes beyond a template, and who would rather work with one team than coordinate between a separate designer and developer. See also the work for [BIYU](/work/biyu) and [A/Café](/work/a-cafe-app-design).

## Frequently asked questions

### Do you come to Amsterdam?

Yes. We regularly work in Amsterdam and are happy to meet at your place, for a first conversation or along the way. After that most of the work runs remotely, with clear updates along the way.

### Can you build and maintain my website in Amsterdam?

Yes. We design, build and maintain websites and stores for Amsterdam brands, from a first version to ongoing improvements after launch. The same team stays involved, so you do not have to explain how your site works every time something changes.

### What work have you done in Amsterdam?

For [BIYU](/work/biyu), which had an office on the Overtoom at the time, we worked on the app, the website, the dashboard and the partner portal. For [A/Café](/work/a-cafe-app-design) we designed the kiosk, the POS and the kitchen display as one ordering system, now running in test phase across their stores, including Amsterdam.

### Do you build Shopify stores for Amsterdam brands?

Yes. As an official Shopify Partner we build and improve [Shopify stores](/shopify-development), from a custom theme to integrations and Shopify Markets when you sell internationally.

### How much does a website cost in Amsterdam?

The price depends on scope, not on the city. The article on [what a website costs](/insights/website-cost) gives realistic ranges for the Dutch market, and a first conversation with us is free and without obligation.

### Do you also work outside Amsterdam?

Yes. We are based in [Almere](/web-design-almere) and work for businesses in Almere and the surrounding area, Amsterdam and Eindhoven, and for clients across the rest of the Netherlands and beyond.
