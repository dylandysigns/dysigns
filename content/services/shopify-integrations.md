## What this includes

### Payments and shipping

iDEAL, Klarna, credit card and the carriers you use, set up and tested before you go live.

### Inventory and accounting

Stock and invoices that add up automatically, so you do not keep two systems in sync by hand.

### Marketplaces and channels

Your products and orders on Bol.com and other channels, without updating everything separately.

### CRM and email

Customer data flowing into your email marketing and CRM, so mailings and customer contact run on real data.

### Custom work through the API

If no app fits what you need, we build a connection through the Shopify API, webhooks or Shopify Flow.

## An app first, custom work second

An existing app is usually faster, cheaper and easier to maintain than a custom connection. So we look at what exists first, and only build something new when it really pays off. A connection nobody can maintain is a risk, not an advantage. We keep that in mind with every choice.

## Who it is for

For growing stores that notice manual work between systems costs time and causes errors. Also for agencies looking for a specialist for the Shopify part of a larger project. For [STËLZ](/work/stelz-web-design) we built a store that moves along with the rest of the brand.

## Frequently asked questions

### Which systems can you connect to Shopify?

Most common ones: accounting packages, inventory and fulfilment systems, CRM, email platforms, carriers and marketplaces. If you have something unusual, just ask and we will see what is possible.

### Can you connect Shopify to Bol.com?

Yes. We connect your store to Bol.com and other marketplaces, so products, stock and orders do not need to be tracked separately on each channel.

### Do you work with existing apps?

Yes, and we prefer it over custom work. We build on what already works and only add where needed.

### When is a custom connection needed?

When no app does what you need, or a combination of apps becomes too expensive or too fragile. Then we build a tailored solution through the Shopify API, webhooks or Flow.

### What happens when a connection breaks?

We test connections before launch and document how they work, so a failure is quicker to find and fix. For ongoing care we can keep maintaining your store.

### What does an integration cost?

An existing app costs a subscription and some setup time. Custom work depends on complexity. After a first conversation you will know where you stand, without obligation.
