## What this includes

### Design

A visual design fitted to your brand and audience, from first concept to a finished, high-fidelity design.

### Development

A fast, working site built from that design, not an approximation of it.

### Custom website

Not an adjusted standard theme, but a website design made for you: structure, design and functionality fitted to your brand and your customers.

### Shopify stores

For online stores specifically, we build on [Shopify](/shopify-development) as an official Shopify Partner, the platform we use most for commerce.

### Redesign and migration

An existing website or store taken over and improved step by step, or moved to a platform that fits better, without losing what already works.

### Performance and technical SEO

Fast on mobile and technically findable from the first release: clean structure, proper metadata and pages search engines can read.

## Why this sits with design and development

A site designed by one vendor and built by another loses something in the handover every time. Here the same team designs and builds it, so what launches is what was actually designed, not a compromise reached under a development deadline.

## Frequently asked questions

### What does a web design agency do?

A web design agency designs and builds websites. At DYSIGNS that covers the full route: the structure and content of the site, the visual design, the development, and the technical side that makes it fast and findable. Because design and development sit with the same team, what goes live is what was designed.

### How long does it take to design and build a website?

A normal website project takes about 8 to 12 weeks from first conversation to launch, depending on scope. A smaller site or landing page goes faster, a large store or a complex integration takes longer. You get a roadmap at the start, so you know what happens when.

### Do you work with companies outside the Netherlands?

Yes. We are a Dutch web design agency based in Almere, and we work with clients in the Netherlands and abroad. Projects run largely remote, in English or Dutch, and for clients nearby we are happy to meet on location.

### Which websites have you designed?

Recent work includes the web design for [Sfumato Agency](/work/sfumato-agency), the Shopify store for [STËLZ](/work/stelz-web-design) and the [Merely Made webshop](/work/merely-made-webshop). You can see all of it on the [work page](/work).

### How much does a website or online store cost?

We do not work with a minimum or a maximum. Every project differs in scope, technology and ambition, so a standard price would only give you the wrong picture. We would rather sit down first and hear what you are trying to achieve. That first conversation is free and comes with no obligation. Want a sense of the ranges first? Read [what a website costs](/insights/website-cost).

### Do you build on an existing platform, or fully custom?

That depends on the project. Shopify, where we build as an official Shopify Partner, is the most common choice for online stores; for large or complex commerce we look at headless options like Medusa; for websites we build custom front end. We decide per project based on your scale, your markets and who maintains it afterwards.

### Can you take over an existing website or store?

Absolutely. You do not need to start over to move forward. We iterate on what you already have: first a look at what is there and what is holding it back, then improvements step by step. That often delivers results faster than a full rebuild.

### Do you only design, or do you build as well?

Both, and that is the point. Design and development sit with the same partner, so nothing gets lost in the handover. If you only need design because you have your own development team, we deliver a full handoff with design system and specifications.

### Is the site also fast and mobile friendly?

Yes. Performance and mobile behaviour are design decisions made from the first concept, not something tested only after the desktop version is already finished.

### Do you work with WordPress?

Yes, when it fits your project. WordPress can be a good choice, for example if you already use it. We look per project at what works best: WordPress, Shopify or a custom website. That way you choose the platform that fits your scale and your team.

### Who owns the website, the design and the code?

You do. Once everything is paid, the design, the code, the content and the accounts are yours. You are not tied to anything and can take it all with you if you want something else later.

### Can I edit text and images myself after launch?

Yes. You can edit text, images and pages yourself, and we show you how it works. Many clients also leave it to us through maintenance, so they do not have to think about it.

### Do you also write the copy and handle photography or video?

Yes, both. We write copy, also with SEO in mind, and handle photography and video. Whatever you need goes into the quote.

### Can you connect to my CRM, accounting or booking system?

Yes. We connect your website or store to the systems you already use, such as a CRM, accounting software or booking system, so data does not have to be copied over by hand.

### Do you handle the cookie banner and the GDPR side of the site?

Yes. We set up a cookie banner and privacy friendly tracking properly, in line with the GDPR.

### Do you work with Webflow or Framer?

Yes, when it fits your project. Just like with WordPress, we look per project at which platform works best for your scale and your team.
