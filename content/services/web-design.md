## What this includes

### Design

A visual design fitted to your brand and audience, from first concept to a finished, high-fidelity design.

### Development

A fast, working site built from that design, not an approximation of it.

### Shopify stores

For online stores specifically, we build on [Shopify](/shopify-development) as an official Shopify Partner, the platform we use most for commerce.

## Why this sits with design and development

A site designed by one vendor and built by another loses something in the handover every time. Here the same team designs and builds it, so what launches is what was actually designed, not a compromise reached under a development deadline.

## Who it is for

For founders, scale-ups and agencies who want a website or online store that goes beyond a template, and who would rather work with one team than coordinate between a designer and a separate developer.

## Frequently asked questions

### How much does a website or online store cost?

We do not work with a minimum or a maximum. Every project differs in scope, technology and ambition, so a standard price would only give you the wrong picture. We would rather sit down first and hear what you are trying to achieve. That first conversation is free and comes with no obligation.

### Do you build on an existing platform, or fully custom?

That depends on the project. Shopify, where we build as an official Shopify Partner, is the most common choice for online stores; for large or complex commerce we look at headless options like Medusa; for websites we build custom front end. We decide per project based on your scale, your markets and who maintains it afterwards.

### Can you take over an existing website or store?

Absolutely. You do not need to start over to move forward. We iterate on what you already have: first a look at what is there and what is holding it back, then improvements step by step. That often delivers results faster than a full rebuild.

### Do you only design, or do you build as well?

Both, and that is the point. Design and development sit with the same partner, so nothing gets lost in the handover. If you only need design because you have your own development team, we deliver a full handoff with design system and specifications.

### Is the site also fast and mobile friendly?

Yes. Performance and mobile behaviour are design decisions made from the first concept, not something tested only after the desktop version is already finished.
