## What this includes

### Custom theme

A Shopify theme fitted to your brand, not an adjusted default template.

### Custom functionality

Liquid sections and apps built for what your store specifically needs.

### Optimisation and maintenance

A store that keeps performing after launch, with room to keep developing it further.

### Integrations and apps

Payment providers, inventory, accounting or CRM: we connect what your store needs and build on apps that already work.

### Migration to Shopify

Moving over from another platform, with product and order data carried across and custom logic rebuilt where Shopify has no standard equivalent.

### Selling internationally

Shopify Markets for multiple languages, currencies and countries, set up the way Shopify intends.

## When Shopify is not the right choice

Shopify is strong for most e-commerce use cases, but it is not always the best fit, for example with very specific backend integrations or once platform costs at high volume start outweighing the convenience. We give an honest recommendation when another platform suits you better, even if that means we do not start a Shopify project.

## Who it is for

For founders and agencies with a Shopify store that needs to go beyond a standard theme, or who want an existing store improved rather than rebuilt. ## Frequently asked questions

### Are you an official Shopify Partner?

Yes. DYSIGNS is a registered Shopify Partner, which means we work with Shopify's own tooling, apps, and Markets setup the way Shopify intends, not around it, and we stay current as the platform changes.

### What does an e-commerce agency do?

An e-commerce agency designs, builds and improves online stores, and makes sure the technology behind them is right: the platform choice, payments, integrations with inventory and accounting, speed and findability. DYSIGNS does that mainly on Shopify, as an official Shopify Partner, with design and development in one team. If Shopify does not fit your situation, we say so honestly and recommend another platform.

### What does a custom Shopify store mean?

Your store does not run on a standard theme with a few tweaks, but on a theme, sections and functionality designed and built for your brand and products. Think of your own product and collection page structure, Liquid sections your team can fill in themselves, or integrations with your inventory or accounting system. You keep what Shopify does well, such as hosting, security and payments, while the store looks and works like yours.

### Why hire a Shopify agency instead of using a standard theme?

A good standard theme is fine to start with. An agency pays off once your store has to stand out, needs specific functionality or has to talk to other systems. At DYSIGNS design and development sit in one team, so what gets designed is built exactly that way. Not sure Shopify fits? Read [Shopify vs. a custom-built site](/insights/shopify-vs-custom-website).

### How long does it take to build a Shopify store?

As a guideline a project takes 8 to 12 weeks, depending on scope, integrations and how quickly copy, product data and photography are ready. Improving an existing store is often faster.

### Can you move my current store to Shopify?

Yes. Product and order data migrates cleanly in most cases. The bigger work is usually rebuilding custom logic that has no standard Shopify equivalent.

### Can an existing Shopify store be improved, or does it need a rebuild?

Often an existing store can be improved without rebuilding everything, depending on the current technical state of the theme and the apps already in use.

### How much does a Shopify store cost?

It depends on scope and integrations. As a general market indication, a professionally designed Shopify store typically starts around €6,000 to €8,000, and a custom project often runs between €10,000 and €30,000 or more, plus the platform subscription and apps. The full breakdown is in [what a website or webshop costs](/insights/website-cost).

### Do you work with existing Shopify apps and integrations?

Yes, we build on what already works and add where needed, instead of replacing everything by default.

### Do you support multiple languages or countries through Shopify Markets?

Yes, Shopify Markets is part of what we implement when a store needs to sell internationally.
