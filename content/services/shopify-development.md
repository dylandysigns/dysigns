## What this includes

### Custom theme

A Shopify theme fitted to your brand, not an adjusted default template.

### Custom functionality

Liquid sections and apps built for what your store specifically needs.

### Optimisation and maintenance

A store that keeps performing after launch, with room to keep developing it further.

### Integrations and apps

Payment providers, inventory, accounting or CRM: we connect what your store needs and build on apps that already work.

### Migration to Shopify

Moving over from another platform, with product and order data carried across and custom logic rebuilt where Shopify has no standard equivalent.

### Selling internationally

Shopify Markets for multiple languages, currencies and countries, set up the way Shopify intends.

### A store your team runs itself

Sections with settings, or a visual page builder such as Instant, so your team changes heroes, campaigns and products without waiting for a developer.

### In-house apps instead of paid plugins

Shopify covers a lot out of the box, but every growing store hits its limits. Rather than stacking paid third-party apps, we build what you need ourselves: gifts with a purchase, bundles and upsells, deposits, order limits checked on the server. You own it, without monthly subscriptions.

### Conversion and mobile

Most visitors shop on their phone, so that is where we start: a sticky add-to-cart button, quick add on every product, upsells in the cart and a checkout without friction.

### Shopify Plus

For brands that outgrow the standard plans: a checkout you can customise, extra stores per country, brand or wholesale channel, B2B at scale and campaigns you schedule ahead. We tell you honestly when Plus pays off and when a regular plan is enough.

### Headless Shopify

A custom storefront on Shopify's Storefront API, built with Hydrogen or Next.js, for when a theme can no longer carry the experience. Shopify stays the engine for products, checkout and payments. A well-built custom theme is often faster to launch and cheaper to run, and we say so when it is.

### Shopify B2B

Business customers who order themselves, with company accounts, their own price lists and payment terms, from the same store your consumers shop in.

## When Shopify is not the right choice

Shopify is strong for most e-commerce use cases, but it is not always the best fit, for example with very specific backend integrations or once platform costs at high volume start outweighing the convenience. We give an honest recommendation when another platform suits you better, even if that means we do not start a Shopify project.

## Frequently asked questions

### Are you an official Shopify Partner?

Yes. DYSIGNS is a registered Shopify Partner, which means we work with Shopify's own tooling, apps, and Markets setup the way Shopify intends, not around it, and we stay current as the platform changes.

### What does an e-commerce agency do?

An e-commerce agency designs, builds and improves online stores, and makes sure the technology behind them is right: the platform choice, payments, integrations with inventory and accounting, speed and findability. DYSIGNS does that mainly on Shopify, as an official Shopify Partner, with design and development in one team. If Shopify does not fit your situation, we say so honestly and recommend another platform.

### What does a custom Shopify store mean?

Your store does not run on a standard theme with a few tweaks, but on a theme, sections and functionality designed and built for your brand and products. Think of your own product and collection page structure, Liquid sections your team can fill in themselves, or integrations with your inventory or accounting system. You keep what Shopify does well, such as hosting, security and payments, while the store looks and works like yours.

### Why hire a Shopify agency instead of using a standard theme?

A good standard theme is fine to start with. An agency pays off once your store has to stand out, needs specific functionality or has to talk to other systems. At DYSIGNS design and development sit in one team, so what gets designed is built exactly that way. Not sure Shopify fits? Read [Shopify vs. a custom-built site](/insights/shopify-vs-custom-website).

### What do I need to have a store built?

Mostly clarity about what you sell and to whom. Product data (names, prices, variants), good product photos, your brand identity and a view on shipping and returns make the project faster. Payments, hosting and security are handled by Shopify. Whatever is still missing, such as copy or photography, we include in the planning.

### How long does it take to build a Shopify store?

As a guideline a project takes 8 to 12 weeks, depending on scope, integrations and how quickly copy, product data and photography are ready. Improving an existing store is often faster.

### Can you move my current store to Shopify?

Yes. Product and order data migrates cleanly in most cases. The bigger work is usually rebuilding custom logic that has no standard Shopify equivalent.

### Can an existing Shopify store be improved, or does it need a rebuild?

Often an existing store can be improved without rebuilding everything, depending on the current technical state of the theme and the apps already in use.

### What is the difference between Shopify and Shopify Plus?

Both run on the same platform, with the same hosting, security and checkout. Shopify Plus adds what larger and faster-growing brands need: customising the checkout itself, extra stores for other countries, brands or wholesale, scheduled campaigns, more room for B2B and your own apps with custom discount, shipping and payment logic. Upgrading does not make your store better on its own; it widens what you can adapt and automate.

### When do I need Shopify Plus?

Usually when you want to change the checkout beyond the standard settings, run several stores for different countries, brands or wholesale, sell B2B with many customer groups and price lists, or when your revenue is high enough that the lower payment rates on Plus offset the plan price. If none of that applies, a regular Shopify plan is often the better choice, and the difference is better spent on the store itself.

### What is headless Shopify, and when is it worth it?

In a headless setup Shopify stays the engine for products, cart, checkout and payments, while the storefront becomes its own application on the Storefront API, built with Hydrogen or Next.js. It pays off when your experience, content model or performance needs go beyond what a theme can do. It also means more to maintain: hosting, updates and replacements for apps that only work in a theme. For most brands a well-built custom theme is the faster and cheaper route, and we tell you when that is the case.

### Why build your own apps instead of using App Store apps?

App Store apps are a good start, but each one adds a monthly fee, extra scripts that slow the store down and logic you do not control. For STËLZ we built gifts with a purchase, upsells and bundles, deposits and minimum and maximum order quantities ourselves, with the order limits checked on the server so they always hold. You get exactly what your store needs, it is yours, and there are no extra subscriptions.

### Can my team manage the store without a developer?

Yes. We build the store from sections with settings, or set it up with a visual page builder such as Instant, so your team changes heroes, campaigns, collections and products itself. For brands that run a lot of campaigns, a new promotion goes live in minutes.

### How does a Shopify project with DYSIGNS run?

In five steps. We start with your goals, products, systems and what has to carry over from your current store. Then we design the store and decide at the same time how it connects to your other systems. We build in reviewable parts, migrate your data and test templates, devices, payments, tracking and redirects before launch. We go live without downtime, keeping old URLs working. After launch we keep improving the store and act on your campaigns.

### How much does a Shopify store cost?

It depends on scope and integrations. As a general market indication, a professionally designed Shopify store typically starts around €6,000 to €8,000, and a custom project often runs between €10,000 and €30,000 or more, plus the platform subscription and apps. The full breakdown is in [what a website or webshop costs](/insights/website-cost).

Yes, we build on what already works and add where needed, instead of replacing everything by default.

Yes, Shopify Markets is part of what we implement when a store needs to sell internationally.

### Do you also build B2B stores or subscriptions?
