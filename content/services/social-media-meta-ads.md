## Frequently asked questions

### How much does social media and Meta Ads management cost?

We do not work with a minimum or a maximum. Every engagement differs in scope and ad spend, so a standard price would only give you the wrong picture. We would rather sit down first and hear what you are trying to achieve. That first conversation is free and comes with no obligation.

### How is ad spend handled?

Ad spend sits separately from our fee and stays under your control at all times. We plan and manage campaigns; the budget itself is never bundled into what you pay us.

### How long before a campaign shows real results?

We start with a three month period, because campaigns below that simply do not gather enough data to steer on. Early weeks are for testing; the following months are where we act on what the data actually shows.

### Do you take over an existing social media presence?

Yes. We look at what is already there and what is holding it back before we propose changes, rather than starting from a blank page by default.

### Can you run ads without also building the landing page?

Yes, though we recommend at least reviewing it, since a campaign only performs as well as the page it sends people to. If you already have a page that converts, we work with what exists.
