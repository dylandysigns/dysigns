## What this includes

### Workflow assessment

A clear look at where AI could realistically save time in your business, site or product, and where it would just add complexity.

### AI creatives

Campaign visuals, product images in new settings, ad variations and short video, made with AI within your brand identity, and always checked by a designer before anything goes live.

### Improving processes with AI

Processes that now depend on one person's knowledge, such as returns, quotes or onboarding, turned into clear steps that AI helps carry out, so the whole team can handle them.

### Automation across the business

Repetitive work between systems taken over by workflows, for example with n8n: invoices, stock, leads and reports, with AI handling the steps that need judgement.

### Dashboards and business insight

A business dashboard in your own brand style, with the numbers from your store, ads, Google Analytics, email, accounting and stock. Every week a short AI summary tells you what changed and what needs attention.

### AI chat and assistants

An assistant on your website that answers visitors from your own content, or an internal assistant that answers your team from your documents and process agreements.

### Implementation and handoff

Built into the actual workflow and tested with your team, then documented and trained, so it keeps working after we step back.

## Why this sits with design and development

An AI feature that ignores how the rest of your product looks and behaves gets used once and ignored after that. Because the same team designs the interface around it, an AI implementation here fits the product it lives in instead of feeling bolted on.

## Who it is for

For founders, scale-ups and agencies who suspect AI could save real time somewhere in their product or workflow, but want an honest assessment before committing budget to it.

## Use cases

### A dashboard for the whole business

Revenue, orders, ad spend, email revenue, margin and stock in one view, refreshed automatically and in your own brand style. Every week an AI summary tells you what changed and what needs attention, so the weekly meeting starts with the right questions.

### A returns assistant for the team

A new colleague does not know how the returns process works across warehouses and exceptions. They ask the internal assistant and get the right steps straight away, based on your own process agreements.

### Customer service that sorts itself

Incoming emails are read and sorted automatically. Questions such as "where is my order?" get an answer straight away, and only the cases that need judgement end up with a person.

### AI creatives for a campaign

From one product shoot, a set of campaign visuals in different settings, formats and variations for ads, made within your brand identity and checked by a designer.

Descriptions, tags and metadata for hundreds of products, written in your tone of voice and kept consistent, so the catalogue never lags behind again.

### Invoices and quotes without manual work

Supplier invoices matched to orders automatically, with only the real differences flagged, and first drafts of quotes prepared from an intake, ready for a final check.

## Frequently asked questions

### How much does an AI implementation cost?

We do not work with a minimum or a maximum. Every project differs in scope and complexity, so a standard price would only give you the wrong picture. We would rather sit down first and hear what you are trying to achieve. That first conversation is free and comes with no obligation.

### How do you approach an AI implementation?

In five steps. It starts with a free intro call, where we hear which work takes too much time. Then we map the workflow, including the exceptions that are not written down anywhere, and decide where AI really helps and where it does not. Next we build a first version on one workflow and test it with your team, usually within 4 to 8 weeks, with a person staying in the loop for the cases that need judgement. Once it works, we document it and train your team, so it does not depend on us. After that we maintain it for a fixed monthly fee and expand step by step.

### Do you use Claude, ChatGPT or Gemini?

We work with a range of AI models and choose per project what fits your goal, your budget and your data best. Which solution that becomes for you is something we are happy to discuss in person. [Get in touch](/contact) for a no-obligation conversation.

### What happens to our data?

That is an important question with AI, and the rules and options change quickly. So for every project we look at the regulations that apply at that moment, such as the GDPR and the EU AI Act, and at the provider's terms. We stay up to date with what is happening and adapt our approach to it. And we only send along what a task really needs.

### What's a typical AI automation you build?

Think of a webshop where someone spends an hour every morning matching incoming supplier invoices to purchase orders by hand, or a support inbox where the same five questions get answered over and over in slightly different words. Those are the projects that pay for themselves fastest: the invoice gets matched automatically and only flagged when the numbers genuinely disagree, the recurring questions get answered instantly and only the truly new ones reach a person. Nothing exotic, just time nobody should still be spending.

### What AI automations do you build for e-commerce?

Four patterns come up again and again. Customer service triage: incoming questions get read, categorised and routed automatically, so a person only sees the ones that genuinely need judgment, not the ten "where is my order" messages in between. Dynamic product enrichment: descriptions, tags and metadata get generated and kept consistent across hundreds of SKUs, instead of a spreadsheet nobody has time to update. Automated lead nurturing: a lead's behaviour triggers the right follow-up in a tool like Klaviyo, instead of someone manually building every segment by hand. Stock notifications: low-inventory and back-in-stock alerts fire automatically through a workflow tool like n8n, connected straight to the store and the team's channels. The return is the same across all four: hours back every week, fewer manual errors, and systems that actually talk to each other instead of three separate logins.

### Can AI just replace what a creative partner like you does?

Parts of it, certainly, and we use those tools daily ourselves. We are not better than AI, nobody is. The difference is knowing what to do with it: which problem you are solving, what you hand to a model and what you should deliberately not automate.

### Do you only take on the straightforward cases?

No, often the opposite. In one project a client's return process ran across two warehouses, three languages, and a list of manual exceptions nobody had ever written down; a lot of agencies would automate the easy 80% and quietly leave the rest. We mapped the real exception rules first, built the automation around what was actually repeatable, and kept a person in the loop for the handful of cases that genuinely needed judgment. Messy, undocumented processes are usually where automation saves the most time, not the least.

### Will you tell me if AI is not the right fit?

Yes. Part of the assessment is being honest when a workflow is not a good candidate for AI yet, rather than implementing something just because it was asked for.

### Do I need existing AI infrastructure before we start?

No. We assess what you have and recommend an approach that fits it, whether that means starting from nothing or working with tools already in place.

### Do you maintain the implementation after launch?

Yes. After launch we maintain the implementation for a fixed monthly fee: we keep an eye on whether everything keeps working, adjust it when your processes change and roll out updates.

### How soon do I see a first working result?

Usually within 4 to 8 weeks. In that time we map the workflow, build the first version and test it with your team. After that we expand step by step.

### Can you build an AI chat for my website?

Yes. An assistant that gives visitors an answer straight away from your own content, such as your FAQ, services and products. The chat on this site is an example of that. It does not make things up, and when it does not know something, it refers the visitor to you.

Yes. Incoming questions are read, sorted and, where the answer is clear, answered straight away. Your team only sees the questions that really need a person, and every answer stays in your tone of voice.

Often with a workflow tool such as n8n, which links your store, inbox, CRM and other systems together. AI handles the steps that need judgement, such as reading an email or recognising an exception, and the workflow takes care of the rest automatically.

Yes. Think of campaign visuals, product images in new settings, ad variations and short video. We work within your brand identity, and a designer checks everything before it goes live, so it looks like your brand and not like generic AI.

### Can you build a dashboard for our whole business?

Yes. We build a business dashboard in your own brand style, connected to the systems you already use, such as Shopify, Meta and Google Ads, Google Analytics, Klaviyo, your accounting and your stock. ### How soon is my dashboard live?

A first version is usually live within 4 to 8 weeks. After that we expand it step by step with the numbers you are still missing.

### Is a business dashboard also useful for service businesses?

Yes. Besides the basics, such as your accounting, ads and Google Analytics, we also connect your CRM, your calendar or planning and your time tracking. That way you also see your leads, your occupancy and your profitability per client in one view.

### What is the difference between automation and AI?

Automation follows fixed rules: if this happens, do that. AI can handle what does not fit a fixed rule, such as reading an email, recognising an exception or writing a first draft. Most of our solutions combine the two: automation for the steps that are always the same, AI for the steps that need judgement.

### Is AI also worth it for a small business?

Yes, small teams in particular often save a lot of time with it. We then start with one workflow that pays for itself quickly, and only expand once that works.

### Can you train our team to work with AI?

Yes. We run trainings and workshops in which your team learns to work with AI tools, tailored to your own work and processes.
