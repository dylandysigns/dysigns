## What this includes

### Audit and plan

We start with what you have now: products, variants, apps, integrations and custom features. From that comes a plan of what can move across, what needs to change and what drops away.

### Migrating the data

Products, customers and orders move to Shopify, with care for variants, collections and customer accounts. We test the import on a staging environment first.

### Keeping SEO and URLs

Every old URL gets a destination. We set up redirects, carry over metadata and structured data, and check after launch that Google keeps finding everything.

### Go-live and aftercare

A fixed switch date, a test round of the whole purchase flow and a fallback plan. After launch we stay available for whatever turns up.

## When migrating is not worth it

A migration costs time and money, and not every store gets better from it. If your current platform runs well, costs are reasonable and you are not missing features, improving it is often smarter than switching. If your store depends on very specific integrations Shopify cannot replicate, we tell you up front. We only migrate when it genuinely moves your store forward.

## Frequently asked questions

### Will my products, customers and orders move to Shopify?

In most cases, yes. Products, customers and order history can be imported. Most of the work usually goes into rebuilding custom logic that has no standard equivalent on Shopify.

### Will I lose my Google rankings in a migration?

That is the risk we take most seriously. With a full URL mapping, redirects and carried-over metadata, a small fluctuation is normal, but a lasting drop can be avoided. After launch we follow how your pages perform.

### How long does a migration to Shopify take?

As a guideline a few weeks to a couple of months, depending on the number of products, integrations and custom features, and how quickly copy and data are ready. The audit gives a more realistic picture.

### Which platforms can you migrate from?

The common ones, such as WooCommerce, Magento, PrestaShop and custom solutions. The technology differs per platform, but the steps stay the same.

### Can my store stay open in the meantime?

Yes. We build the new store alongside the old one and only switch on the agreed date, so your sales do not stall.

### What does a migration to Shopify cost?

It depends on the size of your catalogue, the number of integrations and how much custom work is needed. A range for the Dutch market is in [what a website or store costs](/insights/website-cost). After a first conversation we give a concrete proposal.
