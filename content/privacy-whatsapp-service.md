DYSIGNS builds and runs an assistant on WhatsApp for cleaning companies. Cleaners use it to call in sick and to ask questions about their work, in their own language. This page covers the data that passes through that assistant.

**Who is responsible.** The cleaning company (the employer) is responsible for its employees' data. DYSIGNS only processes that data on the company's behalf, under a data processing agreement. If you work for a cleaning company that uses the assistant, your employer is your first point of contact. You may also email us; we will pass your request on.

**What data.** Your name, your phone number, the language you write in, your location or shift, the day you are sick from, whether your report arrived and was seen, a note if you add one yourself, and the questions you ask.

**What we do not keep.** We never ask what is wrong or why you are sick. If you mention it anyway, it is removed before anything is stored or passed on. We do not keep photos. A voice message is transcribed to text; the audio is deleted straight away.

**Who sees it.** Your employer's planner and office. DYSIGNS only looks when that is needed to fix a fault. Your colleagues do not see your report.

**Which services are involved.** Meta (WhatsApp) sends and receives the messages. Anthropic helps the assistant understand a message when needed; this data is not used to train AI models. Cloudflare runs the assistant and stores the data, in the European Union. Meta and Anthropic may process data outside the European Union, under the safeguards the GDPR requires for that.

**How long.** Individual messages are deleted automatically after 30 days. A report (who, where, from when) is kept for 12 months, or shorter if your employer has set it that way.

## Deleting your data

Do you want your data removed from the WhatsApp assistant, or see what is stored about you?

1. Ask your employer's office, or email [info@dylandysigns.com](mailto:info@dylandysigns.com).
2. Include the phone number you use with the assistant and the name of your employer.
3. We check that the request really comes from you, and delete your data within 30 days. You get a confirmation once it is done.

What your employer has to keep about your absence by law is up to your employer. That is separate from the assistant.

## Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to access, correct or delete your data, to object, and to request a copy. Email [info@dylandysigns.com](mailto:info@dylandysigns.com) or ask your employer's office. You may also file a complaint with the Dutch Data Protection Authority (Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens).

For anything that is not about the WhatsApp assistant, see our [general privacy policy](/privacy).
