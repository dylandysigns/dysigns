DYSIGNS builds and runs assistants on WhatsApp for businesses. Their employees or customers use them to report something, such as calling in sick, and to ask questions, in their own language. This page covers the data that passes through those assistants.

**Who is responsible.** The business that uses the assistant is responsible for the data of the people who message it. DYSIGNS only processes that data on the business's behalf, under a data processing agreement. If you message an assistant that DYSIGNS runs, the business behind it is your first point of contact. You may also email us; we will pass your request on.

**What data.** Your name, your phone number, the language you write in, the messages you send and the questions you ask. If you make a report, also its details (for example your location or shift and the date it is about), a note if you add one yourself, and whether your report arrived and was seen.

**What we do not keep.** We never ask for health details, such as what is wrong or why you are sick. If you mention them anyway, they are removed before anything is stored or passed on. We do not keep photos. A voice message is transcribed to text; the audio is deleted straight away.

**Who sees it.** The people at the business who handle your messages, such as a planner, the office or customer service. DYSIGNS only looks when that is needed to fix a fault. Colleagues and other customers do not see your messages.

**Which services are involved.** Meta (WhatsApp) sends and receives the messages. Anthropic helps the assistant understand a message when needed; this data is not used to train AI models. Cloudflare runs the assistant and stores the data, in the European Union. Meta and Anthropic may process data outside the European Union, under the safeguards the GDPR requires for that.

**How long.** Individual messages are deleted automatically after 30 days. A report (who, what, from when) is kept for 12 months, or shorter if the business has set it that way.

## Deleting your data

Do you want your data removed from the WhatsApp assistant, or see what is stored about you?

1. Ask the business behind the assistant, or email [info@dylandysigns.com](mailto:info@dylandysigns.com).
2. Include the phone number you use with the assistant and the name of that business.
3. We check that the request really comes from you, and delete your data within 30 days. You get a confirmation once it is done.

What a business has to keep by law, for example about absence from work, is up to that business. That is separate from the assistant.

## Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to access, correct or delete your data, to object, and to request a copy. Email [info@dylandysigns.com](mailto:info@dylandysigns.com) or ask the business behind the assistant. You may also file a complaint with the Dutch Data Protection Authority (Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens).

For anything that is not about the WhatsApp assistant, see our [general privacy policy](/privacy).
